Every piece begins on our own factory floor in Rajasthan — where seasoned wood, precision machines and the hands of master artisans come together under one roof.

The Craft

Made by hand,
meant to last.

We craft using high-quality, responsibly sourced Sheesham — kiln-dried for stability, hand-joined for strength, and finished by artisans who have spent a lifetime perfecting their trade.

It is this blend of traditional joinery and modern precision that lets every RJ Arts piece look stunning today and stay solid for decades.

01 — Shaping

Precision & Design

Seasoned Sheesham is cut and carved with precision machinery, translating each design into clean, accurate joinery.

02 — Building

Hand Assembly

Master craftsmen join every frame by hand — mortise, tenon and dowel — for furniture that stays rock-steady for generations.

03 — Finishing

Sanding & Polish

Each surface is sanded smooth and hand-polished to bring out the natural grain, then quality-checked before it ships.
