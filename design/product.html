Care Instructions
Cleaning
  • Wipe dust with a soft cotton cloth.
  • Use a slightly moistened cloth, avoiding excess water.
  • Clean only with water — avoid chemicals or harsh cleaners.
Avoid Chemicals
  • Do not use chemical-based cleaners or polyurethane paints.
  • Chemicals can damage the natural finish and reduce durability.
Surface Care
  • Use coasters or mats for hot, cold, or wet items to protect the polish.
  • Wipe spills immediately with a soft, dry cloth to prevent stains.
  • For scratches, burns, or residue, use fine-grit sandpaper, then reapply mineral oil.
Additional Tips
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight as it may fade the polish/colour.
  • Do not drag furniture (especially beds) while repositioning, as it may weaken joints.
